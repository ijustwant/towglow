TowGlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

TowGlow is an app for the TowGlow trailer light tester. The app is developed and published by Heidtoni AS, Norway.

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does

  • The app talks to a TowGlow box near you over Bluetooth Low Energy to switch trailer lights on and off, and to update the box's firmware.
  • The link is encrypted, and the box is paired with a code.
  • The app remembers which box you last used, and keeps this only on your phone.
  • The app works without internet. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: used to find and control your TowGlow box.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): older Android versions require the location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app never reads or stores your location.

Test reports (TowGlow PRO)

PDF test reports are created on your phone. They are shared only when you choose to share them yourself, using your phone's share menu.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts processing personal data, this policy will be updated before that change is released. Examples are purchases handled by Google Play or the App Store, or an optional business subscription with cloud storage.
